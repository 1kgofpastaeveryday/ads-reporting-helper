Ads Reporting Helper

Ads Reporting Helper is a private command-line tool run by a single developer. It reads advertising and analytics reports from Google Ads and Google Analytics so the developer can review campaign performance and prepare reports.

What it does

  • Reads campaign, keyword and search-term reports from Google Ads accounts the developer is authorized to manage.
  • Reads aggregated reports from Google Analytics properties the developer is authorized to view.

The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is used only with the developer's own Google account.

Privacy policy

Ads Reporting Helper は、開発者本人だけが使う非公開のコマンドラインツールです。開発者が権限を持つ Google 広告と Google アナリティクスのレポートを読み取り、成果の確認と報告書の作成に使います。一般には提供していません。
